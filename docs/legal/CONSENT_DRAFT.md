# Consent and notice texts

> ⚠️ **DRAFT — requires lawyer review.** Prepared against the Law of the Republic of Armenia on Personal Data Protection. No article numbers are cited on purpose; the lawyer should add them and align the terms ("data processor", "authorised person", etc.) with the law's own definitions.

## Questions for the lawyer
1. For employee data, is the legal basis the employer's obligations under labour law, or is employee consent needed? (This draft assumes labour-law obligations, with a notice to employees.)
2. Is birth date (used only to detect minors) acceptable under data minimisation, or should we store only an "under 18" flag?
3. Must the Personal Data Protection Agency be notified, and by whom (the customer, Tesuch or both)?
4. Is storage in an EU-region data centre a cross-border transfer requiring extra steps?
