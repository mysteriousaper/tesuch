# Ideal customer profile

## Primary ICP: accounting and outsourcing firms
| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Type | Accounting, payroll or HR-outsourcing firm in Armenia |
| Size | 10+ client companies (sweet spot 20–50) |
| Staff | 2–15 accountants; at least one handles HR paperwork for clients |
| Tools today | ArmSoft (or similar) for accounting/payroll; Excel for HR deadlines |
| Location | Yerevan first, then Gyumri and Vanadzor |
| Pain | Responsible for many clients' HR paperwork, but there is no single view of deadlines or risks. One inspection problem at a client damages the firm's reputation. |
| Trigger events | Inspection at a client; new SRC e-contract requirements; losing an HR accountant; onboarding many new clients |
| Buyer | Firm partner or owner |
| User | HR accountant |
| Why they pay | Fewer emergencies, a sellable "inspection-ready" service for their clients, time saved |

**Disqualifiers:** fewer than 5 client companies; firms that do no HR work for clients.

## Secondary ICP: SMEs with 10–100 staff
| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Sectors | Retail, hospitality, IT services, light manufacturing, clinics |
| HR | No dedicated lawyer; the owner or a part-time accountant handles HR |
| Channel | Telegram-first; reached through their accounting firm or self-sign-up from the free self-check |
| Pain | Doesn't know what the Labour Code requires; fears surprise inspections |
| Why they use it | Weekly Telegram digest with 1–3 concrete fixes |

## Positioning
**For** accounting firms that handle HR for many Armenian companies, **Tesuch** is an inspection-readiness dashboard that **shows which clients would fail a labour inspection and what to fix first**. **Unlike** ArmSoft or Excel, it tracks legal deadlines per employee across all clients and alerts before they're missed.
