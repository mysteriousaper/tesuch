# Building the prospect list

**Rules:** list **companies, not private individuals**. Use only public business information, and no scraping or bulk collection of personal data. Contact people only through company channels (published office email or phone, the website contact form, the company's LinkedIn page) or after a warm introduction. Keep the list in a private spreadsheet, not in the product.

## Public sources
| Source | What to look for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| spyur.am | Categories "Accounting services", "Audit", "Consulting", "HR / recruitment" | Business directory; company name, website, office contacts |
| Accountants' professional association (Armenia) | Member firm lists, events, training sessions | Partner channel; speak at events rather than cold-list members |
| LinkedIn company pages | Firms with "accounting", "outsourcing", "payroll" in Armenia | Use company pages; connect with people only through normal, manual networking |
| Facebook and Telegram groups for Armenian accountants | Recurring questions about the Labour Code, inspections, SRC e-contracts | Join, contribute useful answers, share the free self-check. **No mass DMs.** |
| Firm websites | Services list mentions "HR", "կադրային գործավարություն", "кадровое делопроизводство" | Strong signal of fit |
| Events (tax/accounting conferences, webinars) | Sponsors and speakers | Warm intros |

**Status values:** Not contacted → Contacted → Replied → Demo booked → Pilot → Active → Lost (reason).

## Prioritising
1. Firm offers HR or payroll outsourcing (must have).
2. 10+ client companies (from their website, "our clients" logos, or the first call).
3. Active in accountant communities (warm channel).
4. Warm intro available (partner lawyer, existing customer).

Target for month one: 60 qualified firms on the list, 25 contacted, 10 demos, 3–5 pilots.
